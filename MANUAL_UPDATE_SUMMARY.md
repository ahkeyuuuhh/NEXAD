## 📱 Student Manual - Virtual Consultation

### Step 1: Download Jitsi Meet (Required)
- **Requirement:** Students MUST download Jitsi Meet
- **iOS Link:** https://apps.apple.com/app/jitsi-meet/id1165103905
- **Android Link:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.jitsi.meet
- **Reason:** Better video quality, audio clarity, and connection stability

### Step 2: Request Virtual Consultation
- Select "Virtual" consultation type
- Choose date, time, and describe topic

### Step 3: Prepare for Your Session
- Test camera and microphone
- Find quiet, well-lit location
- Prepare questions/materials
- Ensure stable internet

### Step 4: Join the Virtual Meeting
- Go to "My Consultations"
- Tap "Join Virtual Meeting"
- Jitsi Meet opens automatically

### Step 5: During the Consultation
- Toggle camera/microphone
- Use chat for links/notes
- Share screen if needed
- Use raise hand feature

### Step 6: After the Session
- Review notes and action items
- Schedule follow-ups if needed

## 👨‍🏫 Teacher Manual - Virtual Consultation

### Step 1: Download Jitsi Meet (Recommended)
- **Recommendation:** Teachers should download Jitsi Meet
- **iOS Link:** https://apps.apple.com/app/jitsi-meet/id1165103905
- **Android Link:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.jitsi.meet
- **Note:** Inform students to download it too

### Step 2: Review Virtual Consultation Requests
- Check consultation inbox
- Review student's topic and preferred time

### Step 3: Approve and Schedule
- Approve or suggest alternative time
- System creates unique Jitsi room automatically

### Step 4: Prepare Your Environment
- Test camera, microphone, speakers
- Choose professional background
- Review consultation topic
- Prepare materials
- Ensure stable internet

### Step 5: Start the Virtual Meeting
- Tap "Start Virtual Meeting"
- Jitsi Meet opens with private room
- Wait for student to join

### Step 6: Conduct the Session
Professional features available:
- **Screen sharing** - Share presentations/documents
- **Chat** - Send links, notes, resources
- **Recording** - Record with consent
- **Mute controls** - Manage audio
- **Virtual backgrounds** - Maintain professionalism

### Step 7: Document and Follow Up
- Add detailed notes
- Document action items
- Schedule follow-ups
- Track student progress

### Step 8: Best Practices
- Start meetings on time
- Keep sessions focused
- Encourage video use
- Take notes during session
- End with clear action items
- Remind students about Jitsi Meet
