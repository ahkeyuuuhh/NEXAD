# ✅ Virtual Consultation Manual - Added to Website

## What Was Added

I've added comprehensive Virtual Consultation sections to the NEXAD user manual page for both students and teachers.

## Location

**File:** `nexad-website/manual.html`

## Changes Made

### 1. Student Manual - Virtual Consultation Section

Added a new flow card with 6 detailed steps:

1. **Download Jitsi Meet (Required)**
   - Direct links to iOS App Store and Google Play
   - Explanation of why Jitsi Meet is required
   - Benefits: better video quality, audio clarity, and connection stability

2. **Request Virtual Consultation**
   - How to select "Virtual" consultation type
   - Scheduling and topic selection

3. **Prepare for Your Session**
   - Pre-meeting checklist (camera, microphone, location, internet)

4. **Join the Virtual Meeting**
   - How to access the meeting from "My Consultations"
   - Automatic Jitsi Meet app launch

5. **During the Consultation**
   - Jitsi Meet features overview (camera toggle, chat, screen share, raise hand)

6. **After the Session**
   - Review notes and action items
   - Schedule follow-ups

### 2. Teacher Manual - Virtual Consultation Section

Added a new flow card with 8 detailed steps:

1. **Download Jitsi Meet (Recommended)**
   - Direct links to iOS App Store and Google Play
   - Recommendation to inform students about the app

2. **Review Virtual Consultation Requests**
   - How to check incoming virtual meeting requests

3. **Approve and Schedule**
   - Approval process and automatic room creation

4. **Prepare Your Environment**
   - Professional setup checklist (equipment, background, materials)

5. **Start the Virtual Meeting**
   - How to initiate the meeting from consultations list

6. **Conduct the Session**
   - Professional Jitsi Meet features:
     - Screen sharing
     - Chat functionality
     - Recording (with consent)
     - Mute controls
     - Virtual backgrounds

7. **Document and Follow Up**
   - Post-session note-taking and progress tracking

8. **Best Practices**
   - Professional tips for conducting virtual consultations
   - Time management and engagement strategies
