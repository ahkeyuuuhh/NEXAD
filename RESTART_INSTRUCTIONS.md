# 🔄 SIMPLE RESTART INSTRUCTIONS

## Your OTA update is LIVE! Here's how to see it:

### Step 1: Close the app
- Open recent apps (square button)
- Swipe NEXAD away

### Step 2: Wait 10 seconds
- Just wait a bit

### Step 3: Open NEXAD
- The app downloads the update (5-10 seconds)

### Step 4: Close the app again
- Swipe NEXAD away from recent apps

### Step 5: Open NEXAD
- Done! Changes are now visible

---

## What you'll see:
- Text fields with transparent backgrounds (no white boxes)
- Darker, bolder labels
- White logout icon

## Why restart twice?
- First restart: Downloads update
- Second restart: Applies update

That's it! 🎉
